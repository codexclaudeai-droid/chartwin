# TradingCore Landing Page Spec

## Brand

- Website name: TradingCore
- Chart product name: TC Chart
- Public landing copy should sell the chart subscription service, not expose admin-only operation details.

## Page Order

The landing page must keep this public conversion order:

1. Hero section
2. TC Chart feature highlights
3. Subscription plans
4. FAQ
5. Contact actions: 1:1 support, partnership inquiry, free trial request

## Hero Direction

- Keep the hero visually simple and premium.
- Use a dark navy/blue brokerage-style palette with electric blue or cyan accents.
- Use the candle animation only as a subtle background atmosphere.
- Do not expose access-gate, admin approval queue, or internal service-status cards in the public hero.
- Avoid real-time canvas candle experiments until a stable visual design is approved.

## TC Chart Features

Feature cards should focus on user-facing value from `TradingCore Landing Page Copy.docx`:

- Advanced Algorithmic Signal: multi-indicator cross validation and high-precision buy/sell signals.
- Instant Signal Alert: onsite and Telegram alerts with entry, target, and stop context.
- Professional Charting & Tools: trendline, channel, Fibonacci, position forecast, and measuring tools.
- Backtesting & Stat: historical-data-based backtesting and win-rate statistics.

Do not replace this section with start-route cards or a chart preview block.

## Subscription Plans

- Show plan cards for BASIC, PRO, and ELITE.
- Highlight the 6-month PRO plan as the recommended plan.
- Use the headline: "당신의 트레이딩 성향에 맞는 완벽한 플랜".
- Link users to `/pricing` for the step-by-step subscription checkout.

## FAQ

FAQ should answer the first questions a visitor has before signup:

- Whether beginner traders can use the service.
- Which markets and symbols are supported.
- How signal win-rate and backtesting are verified.
- How free trials work and whether fees are charged.
- How Telegram integration works.
- How subscription cancellation and plan changes work.

## Contact Actions

The bottom conversion area should expose three clear paths:

- 1:1 support inquiry
- Partnership inquiry
- Free trial request through signup/login when required

## Do Not Add

- Workflow/process sections on the public landing page
- Start route cards
- Public access-gate status panels
- Duplicated plan explanations that belong on `/pricing`
- Green/mint accent colors on the final landing design
